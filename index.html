Collection, pre‑processing and annotation.

Order the full pipeline or one stage. Every stage runs on footage we capture or footage you already have.

Collect

Data collection

First-person capture of real tasks at natural pace, on site, by the people who do them every day.

  • Head- or chest-mounted egocentric video
  • Worker narration, translated to English
  • Phones, or the capture rig you specify
  • Custom runs for tasks your robots fail on
Process

Pre-processing

Footage made safe and usable before anyone labels or trains on it.

  • Face and bystander blurring
  • Quality filtering: motion blur, frozen frames, hands out of view
  • Trimming into episodes, de-duplication
  • Transcription and translation of narration
Annotate

Annotation

Labels written to your guidelines, with a second reviewer on every batch.

  • Hand keypoints and pose
  • Timestamped action segments and task steps
  • Object boxes and masks
  • Language captions; success and failure labels

How a project runs.

Start with a scoped sample you can test in your own pipeline, before any volume commitment.

  1. Scope

    You share tasks, environments, hours and label spec. We confirm what we can capture and by when.

  2. Site and consent

    The site owner approves recording. Each worker gives written consent and can say no at no cost.

  3. Capture

    Workers record real shifts and narrate what they do and why.

  4. Process and label

    Blurring, filtering, transcription and annotation, with a second-reviewer pass.

  5. Deliver

    Episodes in your format, each with a manifest and consent record.

Every hour ships with its paperwork.

Buyers now ask where footage came from and who agreed to it. We answer that for every hour we deliver.

Owner-approved sites
We record only where the owner has agreed in writing to the tasks and areas on camera.
Per-worker consent
Each worker signs before recording starts and can stop at any time without it affecting their job.
Paid on top of wages
Workers are paid for every approved hour, in addition to their regular pay.
Bystanders protected
People who did not consent are blurred; formulas, supplier labels and other sensitive areas stay off camera.
DPDP-aware handling
Data is handled with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act in mind and licensed with re-identification barred.
